Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SocialScraper collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the service, you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Content metadata: Post shortcodes, media URLs, captions, and usernames of publicly visible posts you choose to scrape.
  • Usage data: Local statistics about posts scraped, media downloads, and exports, used only to display progress in your dashboard.

2. How We Use Information

All collected information is used exclusively to operate the service: fetching publicly visible content you request, organizing it, and exporting it in the formats you choose. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes. No content is published, scheduled, or posted on your behalf.

3. Third-Party Platforms

Scraping runs inside your own browser session on the connected platform (Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook). Your interactions with those platforms are governed by their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Scraped content is held temporarily in your own browser memory and server environment and protected with standard security practices. We recommend running the service on infrastructure you control.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may clear scraped items, downloads, or exports at any time through the dashboard. Clearing the queue removes the associated metadata from the active configuration. Downloads are stored only where you choose to save them on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the registered developer email associated with your account.
